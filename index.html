Warning (Altitude AMS): Whitney summit is at 14,485 ft (4,415 m). Acclimatize by staying at Lone Pine or Whitney Portal (8,300 ft) the night before. If a group member experiences severe headaches, vomiting, or dizziness, descend immediately with a partner. Do not split up!
